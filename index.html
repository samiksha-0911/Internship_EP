About this model

The page builds a 1,000-email labeled dataset from templates (with some tricky legitimate emails that use words like "free" and "offer"), then trains a multinomial Naive Bayes model with Laplace smoothing on 800 of them. Features are words plus four style signals: links, dollar amounts, repeated exclamation marks and ALL-CAPS words. Because the data is synthetic, treat this as a demonstration of the method, not a production filter.
